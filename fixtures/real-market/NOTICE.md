# Notice: licensed market data

The files in this folder are small slices of real market data, and NWT containers made from them.

- The data comes from a licensed vendor CSV export. It is not public data and it is not synthetic.
- The slices are here so that the examples in this kit show real records. They are kept small: the first
  few hundred or few thousand rows of a file, and one symbol's one-minute bars for one day.
- **Do not redistribute these files, or anything converted from them, without checking the licence** under
  which the data was obtained. Receiving this kit does not give you a licence to the data.
- The data is a sample for learning the format. Do not use it for trading, pricing or any decision.

What each file is, how it was made and how to rebuild it: [docs/REAL-DATA-MARKET.md](../../docs/REAL-DATA-MARKET.md).
`SHA256SUMS` lists the SHA-256 of every source slice and every generated file.
